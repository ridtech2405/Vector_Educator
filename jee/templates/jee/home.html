Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is JEE Main?

JEE Main is the national level engineering entrance examination conducted for admission into NITs, IIITs and other engineering institutes.

❓ What is JEE Advanced?

JEE Advanced is conducted for admission into IITs and is attempted after qualifying JEE Main.

❓ Are Mock Tests Free?

Yes, chapter-wise and full syllabus mock tests are available for students.

❓ Can I Track My Performance?
